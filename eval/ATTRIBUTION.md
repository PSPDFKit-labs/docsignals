# Attribution

## `documents/government-forms/`

Official U.S. federal government forms. As works of the U.S. government, they carry no copyright
(17 U.S.C. § 105) and may be freely redistributed.

Each form was downloaded blank from the issuing agency. A subset of its checkboxes and radio
buttons was then set programmatically. The eval does not use those marks. No text fields were
filled in: every name, date, address, and identification number on these documents is blank
as issued.

| File | Source form | Issuing agency | Original |
|---|---|---|---|
| `va-21p-527ez-pension-application.pdf` | VA Form 21P-527EZ, Application for Veterans Pension | U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs | <https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21P-527EZ-ARE.pdf> |
| `va-21p-534ez-dic-survivors-application.pdf` | VA Form 21P-534EZ, Application for D.I.C., Survivors Pension, and/or Accrued Benefits | U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs | <https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21P-534EZ-ARE.pdf> |
| `irs-2063-departing-alien-statement.pdf` | IRS Form 2063, U.S. Departing Alien Income Tax Statement | Internal Revenue Service | <https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f2063.pdf> |

No real individual's data appears in any of these documents.
