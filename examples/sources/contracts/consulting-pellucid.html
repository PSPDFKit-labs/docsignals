Consulting Agreement

This Consulting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on January 12, 2026 between Pellucid Robotics, Inc. ("Company") and Asha Raman Consulting ("Consultant").

1. Engagement

Company engages Consultant to advise on warehouse automation strategy, including vendor evaluation and rollout planning. Consultant will devote approximately twenty (20) hours per week to the engagement.

2. Compensation

Company will pay Consultant a fee of USD 18,000 per month, payable within fifteen (15) days of each invoice, and will reimburse pre-approved travel expenses.

3. Independent Contractor

Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee of Company. Consultant is responsible for all taxes on amounts paid under this Agreement.

4. Term and Renewal

The initial term is twelve (12) months. This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement only if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice. If Company ends the engagement for any other reason, Company will pay the fees for the remainder of the then-current term.

6. Intellectual Property

All work product that Consultant creates under this Agreement belongs to Company upon full payment.

7. Liability

Company will indemnify Consultant against all claims arising from Company's use of the advice. Company's liability under this Agreement is not limited.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. The parties consent to the jurisdiction of the courts in Santa Clara County, California.
