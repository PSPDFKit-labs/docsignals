Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Brightwater Labs LLC ("Brightwater") and Corvane Systems GmbH ("Corvane") to allow the parties to evaluate a possible business relationship (the "Purpose").

1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public technical or business information that one party discloses to the other, whether in writing, orally, or by demonstration, and that is marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

2. Obligations

The receiving party will hold Confidential Information in confidence, will not disclose it to any third party, and will use it only for the Purpose. The receiving party may share it with employees and advisers who need to know it and who are bound by obligations at least as protective as these.

3. Exclusions

These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, that the receiving party already knew, or that it develops independently.

4. Term

This Agreement expires three (3) years after the date of the last signature below. The obligations for Confidential Information disclosed during the term continue for five (5) years after expiry. This Agreement may not be ended early except by a written amendment executed by both parties.

5. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, excluding its conflict of laws rules. Any dispute will be heard in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware.

6. No Licence

Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any right or licence to the other party's intellectual property.
